San Francisco Bay Region Network Manual Product Accessibility Evaluation

The Expert TA — Accessibility Conformance Report, based on VPAT® Version 2.5Rev, WCAG Edition

This is an independent evaluation, not a vendor self-attestation. A VPAT/ACR is normally completed by the supplier about its own product. This report was produced by the SFBRN Accessible Technology Initiative during a procurement requisition, to determine whether the product meets the CSU's accessibility requirements for acquisition. Every conformance level in it is derived from logged test runs. No claim made by the supplier is reproduced or relied on anywhere in this document. This report is INTERIM: testing is in progress and conformance levels may change. It becomes final when every criterion in scope has been decided from a completed test run, the task walk-throughs have verdicts, and the reviewer of record signs off; the levels below reflect the evidence recorded to date.

Vendor Roadmap

The California State University requires that the information and communication technology it acquires conform to WCAG 2.1 Level AA. This product does not currently meet that standard. Continued failure to meet it may jeopardize future acquisitions. The following fixes should be implemented to meet the WCAG 2.1 AA standard.

Needs Immediate Attention

Level A criteria the product does not meet. Level A is the floor of the standard, and these are the failures that most often stop a person completing a task rather than merely slowing them down.

CriterionLevelIssuesWhat needs to be done

Give each data grid a caption that names it and real column headers. Associate every field with its visible caption. Replace the nested layout tables with CSS layout so spacer cells stop being announced as content, and expose an assignment's due date and duration as text, not only as a coloured bar.

1.4.1 Use of ColorLevel A1Add a second, non-colour cue wherever colour carries meaning: a symbol, an underline or a weight change for randomised values, hint and deduction costs, calendar events and late submission dates. The problem-navigator status marks already pair a symbol with their colour and are the pattern to copy.

The hidden jump-point mechanism does not substitute — it answers "no shortcuts" on the page a student lands on, and its controls have no names.
Class Management — Accessibility Mode, Class Management (standard mode)

Native buttons, links and inputs give them all three at once, which is why this single change clears more failures than any other in this report.
