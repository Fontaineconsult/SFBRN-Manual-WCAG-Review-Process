User Functional Limitations

This product's interface presents usability barriers for people with disabilities. Use the table below to plan equally effective alternatives.

Summary

Of the seven user groups this evaluation covers, five meet barriers in this product; no barrier was found for speech-input users and users with photosensitivity. Blind users are affected most: 5 of the 10 pages evaluated could not be used at all, and 3 of the 22 barriers listed for them stop a task outright rather than slow it down. Of the same 10 pages, low-vision users cannot use 5 and keyboard-only users 3. Of the tasks walked from end to end, “Answer and submit a problem” could not be completed at all and “Open an assignment and read a problem” completed only with barriers; three further tasks have not been walked yet, so this is a floor rather than a full count. The barriers reach 8 of the 10 pages evaluated, so an alternative has to cover whole tasks rather than stand in for a single page.
