The Expert TA Accessibility Conformance Report

Based on VPAT® Version 2.5Rev — WCAG Edition

This is an independent evaluation, not a vendor self-attestation. A VPAT/ACR is normally completed by the supplier about its own product. This report was produced by the evaluating body from its own testing, and every conformance level in it is derived from logged test runs. No claim made by the supplier is reproduced or relied on anywhere in this document. This report is INTERIM: testing is in progress and levels may change.
