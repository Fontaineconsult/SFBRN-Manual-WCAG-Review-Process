Summary

Of the six user groups this evaluation covers, three meet barriers in this product; no barrier was found for deaf and hard-of-hearing users, speech-input users, and keyboard-only users. Blind users are affected most: 0 of the 8 pages evaluated could not be used at all, and 1 of the 1 barriers listed for them stop a task outright rather than slow it down.

4.1.2 Name, Role, Value (Level A) UnderstandingPartially Supports

On the sign-in page every control exposes a correct role and state and is announced on focus ; the one defect is the Password field's name, which includes the Show-password checkbox's label because both sit inside one <label> element.

  • Product-wide — (ONE sentence for the published report: what is wrong and what a user cannot do, present tense, no IDs, no dates, no reviewer, no tool names, ≤ 200 chars — rules in ontology/reporting.md)Blocker
